# Sharing a gallery with a QR code

Once you **publish** a gallery, RawDrive shows a **Share & QR** card right on the gallery page.

## What you get

- A **QR code** for your gallery's public link. Anyone who scans it with a phone camera opens the
  gallery straight away — perfect for a wedding album insert, a welcome card, or a print at the venue.
- The **gallery address** in plain text, with a **copy** button.
- **Download QR** in two formats:
  - **PNG** — a crisp, high-resolution image. Great for WhatsApp, Instagram, or printing on a card.
  - **SVG** — a vector file that stays sharp at *any* size. Use this for large prints, banners, or
    signage.

## How to use it

1. Open your gallery and turn on **Publish**.
2. The **Share & QR** card appears. Your clients can scan the QR to open the gallery.
3. Click **Download QR (PNG)** for everyday sharing, or **SVG (vector)** for large prints.
4. Need just the link? Use the **copy** button next to the address.

> The QR opens the exact same protected link as the Copy button — including the secure key for
> encrypted galleries — so it always opens correctly for your clients.

If you haven't published yet, the card reminds you to publish first (the public link doesn't exist
until then).

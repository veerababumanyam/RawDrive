# Streaming GST Compliance

Feature: F-014 Streaming Commercial v1 (M32–M35).
Jurisdiction: Republic of India — Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
(CGST Act), State GST Acts, Integrated GST Act, and the CGST Rules, 2017.
Document owner: Finance Controller and Platform Eng Lead.
Status: Draft pending CA sign-off.

## Scope

This document classifies the monetised surfaces of the streaming feature
for GST, sets out mandatory invoice contents, place-of-supply rules,
e-invoicing readiness, credit-note handling for refunds, and the audit
trail required for annual return reconciliation.

Monetised surfaces covered:

- **Credit recharge** — prepaid top-up of the streaming credit wallet.
  Invoice is raised at recharge time.
- **Streaming consumption** — metered debit of pre-paid credits at
  stream-publish time. No separate invoice at consumption; the recharge
  invoice is the taxable supply.
- **Rate-card changes** — take effect prospectively; historical credits
  are consumed at their purchase-time rate.
- **Refunds** — issued through a GST credit note referencing the
  original invoice.

Non-monetised surfaces (free trials, promotional credits) are out of
scope; they are tracked separately as marketing expense.

## SAC Code: 998439 at 18%

The streaming service is classified as an Online Information and
Database Access or Retrieval service (OIDAR) for GST purposes.

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| SAC code | **998439** — "Other on-line content n.e.c." |
| Rate | **18%** (CGST 9% + SGST 9% for intra-state; IGST 18% for inter-state) |
| Classification basis | CGST Notification 11/2017 as amended; Schedule of Services |
| Reverse charge | Not applicable for domestic B2B except where the recipient is a non-resident OIDAR customer (s.14 IGST) |

SAC 998439 is applied uniformly across recharge tiers and refunds; the
rate card in `platform_settings` stores amounts inclusive of GST, and
the invoice generator derives the taxable value and tax split from the
gross amount.

## Rule 46 Invoice Required Fields

Every tax invoice generated by the streaming recharge flow complies
with CGST Rule 46. The invoice template embeds the following fields and
the generator fails the build if any are missing.

| Field | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier name | `platform_settings.billing.supplier_name` | RawDrive entity name |
| Supplier address | `platform_settings.billing.supplier_address` | Registered office |
| Supplier GSTIN | `platform_settings.billing.supplier_gstin` | 15-char GSTIN |
| Supplier CIN (optional) | `platform_settings.billing.supplier_cin` | Company identification number, printed when present |
| Invoice number | `invoice_sequence` table | Sequential, FY-scoped, max 16 chars, unique |
| Invoice date | `recharge_invoices.issued_at` | IST date on the invoice face |
| Recipient name | `workspace_billing_profiles.legal_name` | Falls back to workspace display name |
| Recipient address | `workspace_billing_profiles.address` | Required for B2B; required for B2C where invoice value >= INR 50,000 |
| Recipient GSTIN | `workspace_billing_profiles.gstin` | Only for B2B |
| HSN / SAC | Hardcoded `998439` | Always present |
| Description of service | "Streaming credits — <tier> pack" | From rate-card label |
| Quantity / unit | Credits purchased, unit = "credit" | |

## Place of Supply Rules

| Scenario | Place of supply (POS) | Tax split |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B2B, supplier and recipient in same state | Recipient state | CGST + SGST |
| B2B, supplier and recipient in different states | Recipient state | IGST |
| B2C, recipient address on file | Recipient state | CGST + SGST if same state as supplier; IGST otherwise |
| B2C, no address and value < INR 50,000 | Supplier state (s.12(2)(b) IGST) | CGST + SGST |
| OIDAR to non-taxable online recipient outside India | Location of recipient (s.13(12) IGST) | Export of service — zero-rated subject to conditions; otherwise IGST |
| OIDAR to recipient within India who is a non-registered person | Location of recipient | IGST applicable under s.14 IGST |

The invoice generator derives POS from the workspace billing state and
flags any invoice where POS could not be resolved for finance review
before issue.

## E-invoicing Readiness

E-invoicing (IRN + QR from the Invoice Registration Portal) is
mandatory for registered persons whose aggregate turnover in any
preceding financial year from 2017-18 onward exceeded **INR 5 crore**.

Current posture:

- RawDrive is below the threshold in the current FY; e-invoicing is
  **not yet mandatory**.
- The schema is already IRN-ready: `recharge_invoices` carries `irn`,
  `ack_no`, `ack_dt`, and `qr_code` columns (migration 088).
- GSP integration (ClearTax / IRIS) is scoped but disabled behind the
  flag `billing.einvoice_enabled`.
- Turnover is monitored monthly by Finance; crossing INR 4.5 crore
  triggers the readiness checklist:

### Readiness checklist (pre-threshold)

- [ ] GSP contract signed; sandbox credentials in `platform_settings`.
- [ ] IRN generation integration test passes against GSP sandbox.
- [ ] Invoice PDF template prints QR code correctly.
- [ ] Failure-path runbook: what to do when GSP is down.
- [ ] Backfill plan agreed: e-invoicing applies to new invoices only,
      not historical.
- [ ] CA sign-off recorded.

## Reverse Charge Scenarios

Reverse charge on outward supplies of streaming service does not
ordinarily apply. The edge cases to flag for manual review:

| Scenario | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Supply to a SEZ unit or developer | Zero-rated supply; refund under LUT/bond or tax-paid route. Manual review. |
| Supply to government department with TDS under s.51 CGST | Standard rate; recipient deducts TDS and issues TDS certificate; supplier reconciles. |
| Supply to unregistered recipient outside India (OIDAR) | Standard IGST under s.14 IGST; reverse charge not applicable on supplier side. |
| Supplier crosses threshold and becomes "specified service provider" | Reassess — not applicable to OIDAR today. |

## Invoice Retention

Per **Section 36 of the CGST Act**, every registered person must
maintain accounts and invoices for **72 months from the due date of
filing the annual return** for the relevant financial year — rounded
to **8 years** in our internal policy for safety margin.

Storage:

- Primary: Postgres `recharge_invoices` table (migration 088).
- PDF originals: Cloudflare R2 bucket `rawdrive-invoices` with object
  lock + versioning enabled.
- Offsite backup: nightly R2 cross-region replication.
- Deletion: disabled at the bucket policy level; a superuser action
  plus dual approval is required for any deletion — and is itself
  audited in `admin_audit`.

## Refund Treatment — Credit Note SOP

Refunds are always issued via a **GST credit note** referencing the
original invoice so ITC reversal flows correctly through the
recipient's GSTR-2B.

Credit note mandatory fields (CGST Rule 53):

- Credit note number (sequential, separate series from tax invoices).
- Issue date.
- Reference to original invoice number and date.
- Reason (refund, service discontinued, price adjustment).
- Taxable value reduction + tax reduction (CGST/SGST or IGST).
- Signature / DSC.

SOP:

1. Finance identifies refund eligibility (see
   `docs/runbooks/streaming-commercial-v1-rollback.md` Step 4).
2. Ops files credit note via super-admin Billing console; the credit
   note is persisted in `recharge_invoices_credit_notes` with a
   foreign key back to `recharge_invoices`.
3. Payment provider refund is initiated (Razorpay API or PhonePe
   dashboard). The provider refund id is stored on the credit note
   row for reconciliation.
4. Credit note appears in GSTR-1 for the month of issue.
5. Customer receives the credit note PDF + refund receipt email.

Partial refunds are supported; each partial credit note references the
original invoice and records the cumulative refunded amount so the
balance is visible to auditors.

## Audit Trail

Three data objects support the GST audit story:

- `invoice_sequence` — monotonic, FY-scoped counter; prevents invoice
  number gaps and duplicates.
- `recharge_invoices` — append-only (corrections via credit note only);
  `issued_at`, `supplier_state`, `recipient_state`, `pos_state`, and
  tax-split columns are immutable after issue.
- `gst_audit_log` — every invoice issue, credit note issue, IRN
  response, and GSTR export is logged here with actor, timestamp,
  and payload digest.

Access control:

- Only super-admin role `billing-auditor` can export raw GST data.
- Every export writes an `admin_audit` row with the date-range and
  recipient email.
- Re-generation of an invoice PDF is allowed (idempotent, byte-for-byte
  reproducible) but never alters the underlying row.

## Annual Reconciliation (GSTR-9)

The annual return (GSTR-9) is filed by 31 December for the preceding
financial year. Streaming contributions:

1. **GSTR-1 reconciliation** — monthly outward supplies (invoices +
   credit notes) reconcile against `recharge_invoices` and
   `recharge_invoices_credit_notes` exports.
2. **GSTR-3B reconciliation** — tax paid in cash vs. tax payable on
   outward supplies; differences investigated before 20th of the
   following month.
3. **GSTR-9 preparation** — auto-generated HSN summary (SAC 998439
   only), turnover reconciliation with audited financials, and ITC
   reversal schedule (for credit notes).
4. **Auditor's certificate (GSTR-9C)** — prepared by CA if aggregate
   turnover exceeds INR 5 crore.
5. **Discrepancy resolution** — any mismatch triggers a Jira
   `FIN-GST-*` ticket assigned to Finance with a 15-day SLA.

Workflow artefacts:

- `scripts/gst/export-gstr1.sql` — monthly export.
- `scripts/gst/hsn-summary.sql` — HSN/SAC summary for GSTR-9.
- `docs/runbooks/gstr-monthly.md` — month-end close runbook (separate
  document).

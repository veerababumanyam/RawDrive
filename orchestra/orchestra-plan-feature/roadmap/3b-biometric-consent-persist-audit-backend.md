## User story

As a data-protection officer, I want every biometric face-search to require explicit consent and to leave a durable audit record of who searched in which gallery, so special-category processing is consistent and accountable under DPDP/GDPR Art 9.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Make consent a required, persisted, audited precondition on BOTH public biometric endpoints with one shared contract.
- **Summary:** FaceMatch requires consent_given:true but PhotoSearch (same biometric matching) requires none, and neither persists consent. Add an append-only biometric-consent/search audit table + repository, require consent on PhotoSearch, and record a consent+search audit row on both PhotoSearch and FaceMatch (gallery id, timestamp, gated-session subject, match outcome counts) without storing the selfie or its embedding.
- **Out of scope:** The frontend consent step (that is 3c)
- **Out of scope:** Changing the matching algorithm/thresholds (that is 3h)
- **Out of scope:** Persisting selfie images or selfie embeddings (explicitly forbidden by the E2EE posture)

## NFR and compliance

- NFR: Audit write must not block the response on failure paths in a way that loses the request, but a failed audit insert must fail closed (no silent unaudited match).
- NFR: Append-only table; no PII beyond the gated session subject already held.
- Compliance: DPDP/GDPR Art 9: consent required+recorded for special-category biometric processing on every public matching endpoint.

## Rollback

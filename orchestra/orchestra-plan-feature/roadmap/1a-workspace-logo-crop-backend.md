## User story

As a studio owner, when I upload and adjust my business logo on /settings/business, the backend renders a clean WebP brand mark from my chosen crop/zoom/pan and stores it against my workspace so it appears consistently across galleries, invoices, and client emails.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Add workspace-scoped logo upload + crop-render endpoints that mirror the photographer-profile logo pipeline but persist to the workspace branding model. Reuse backend/internal/service/profile_logo.go (RenderLogoCropWebP, normalizeLogoCrop, cropContainAspect) rather than writing a parallel renderer.
- **Out of scope:** Frontend UI (covered by 1b/1c)
- **Out of scope:** Changing the photographer-profile logo endpoints
- **Out of scope:** Changing brand_name / brand_accent_color handling
- **Out of scope:** E2EE — workspace logos are PUBLIC unencrypted brand marks via the avatar-style pipeline

## Dependencies

- None (no upstream sub-feature)
- Module: backend/internal/handler/workspace_profile_handler.go
- Module: backend/internal/handler/routes_m2.go
- Module: backend/internal/service/profile_logo.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GIVEN an authenticated workspace member, WHEN they POST a source image to the new workspace logo upload endpoint, THEN the original is stored as an asset and a free-aspect (object-contain, no square clipping) WebP brand mark is rendered server-side and linked to workspaces.logo_asset_id with logo_metadata populated (filename, content_type, size_bytes, storage_key, storage_driver, and the crop position).
- [ ] GIVEN an existing workspace logo, WHEN they POST a new crop position (x, y in [-1,1], zoom in [1,4]) to the workspace logo crop endpoint, THEN the brand mark is re-rendered from the stored original with the normalized position and workspaces.logo_metadata is updated, WITHOUT requiring a re-upload.
- [ ] GIVEN crop inputs outside the valid range, WHEN the endpoint normalizes them, THEN zoom is clamped to [1,4] and x/y to [-1,1] mirroring normalizeLogoCrop, and out-of-range inputs never error.
- [ ] GIVEN the rendered logo, THEN it is produced via the WebP pipeline (cwebp, alpha preserved) and stored on B2 through the s3 driver — never local disk, no hardcoded credentials.
- [ ] GIVEN a member of another workspace, WHEN they target this workspace's logo endpoints, THEN they are rejected (workspace-scoped authorization), and JWT claims are read via middleware.JWTClaimsFromContext.

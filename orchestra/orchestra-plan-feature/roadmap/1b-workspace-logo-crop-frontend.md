## User story

As a studio owner on /settings/business, when I upload my business logo I can zoom, pan (drag or sliders), preview, and Apply the crop before it is saved — just like avatar/logo croppers in other SaaS apps — instead of the logo being stored exactly as the raw file.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Add a workspace-scoped crop step to the business settings logo upload by reusing the LogoCrop interaction pattern (a WorkspaceLogoCrop component or a parameterized reuse of logo-crop.tsx) wired to the 1a endpoints, replacing the current raw POST /api/v1/assets + PUT flow with a crop-then-commit flow.
- **Out of scope:** The prominent top brand-identity header (covered by 1c)
- **Out of scope:** Backend endpoints (covered by 1a)
- **Out of scope:** Photographer profile crop (already exists, unchanged)

## Dependencies

- Depends on roadmap #1a

- Module: frontend/src/app/(dashboard)/settings/business/_components/
- Module: frontend/src/lib/api/workspace-profile.ts

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GIVEN I click Upload studio logo and pick an image, WHEN the file is selected, THEN an interactive crop step appears with zoom (1–4x), horizontal and vertical pan sliders, drag-to-pan, a live object-contain preview, and Apply / Cancel — reusing the LogoCrop interaction pattern, not a new bespoke one.
- [ ] GIVEN I adjust and click Apply, WHEN the crop commits, THEN the frontend calls the workspace logo upload/crop endpoints from 1a, the rendered WebP brand mark is shown in the existing logo preview, and the page reflects the saved logo without a full reload.
- [ ] GIVEN an existing logo, WHEN I choose to re-adjust, THEN I can re-crop without re-uploading (calls the crop endpoint) and the preview updates.
- [ ] GIVEN the crop UI, THEN all buttons use GlassButton/GlassIconButton with SF Symbol icons, only design-tokens.json semantic classes (no neutral-*/gray-*/arbitrary values), render correctly across liquid-glass, liquid-glass-dark, and midnight themes, and all interactive controls are >=44px touch targets with focusRing tokens.
- [ ] GIVEN Cancel, WHEN pressed, THEN no upload/crop call is made and the prior logo (if any) is unchanged.

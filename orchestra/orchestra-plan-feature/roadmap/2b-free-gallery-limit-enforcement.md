# 2b - Enforce the free gallery limit at gallery authority points

**Status:** PENDING

## User story

As a free workspace owner, I should be stopped at the free gallery limit before creating or publicly sharing more galleries, and as a paid workspace owner I should keep the paid catalog limit.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Enforce the catalog gallery_limit for free workspaces and any future limited plan while respecting -1 as unlimited.
- **Summary:** Add a backend entitlement guard that reads the workspace plan tier and subscription_plans.gallery_limit, counts active live galleries, and blocks create/publish/share transitions that exceed the current catalog limit.
- **Out of scope:** Deleting existing over-limit galleries automatically
- **Out of scope:** Charging or auto-upgrading users at the cap
- **Out of scope:** Changing gallery deletion or archive retention behavior

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A free workspace with three active non-deleted/non-archived galleries receives 403 gallery_limit_exceeded on POST /api/v1/galleries.
- [ ] A free workspace already over the limit cannot newly publish an unpublished gallery or create a share link that would expose another public gallery.
- [ ] A paid workspace whose catalog gallery_limit is -1 can create/publish/share without this cap.
- [ ] The guard reads the current DB-backed subscription_plans.gallery_limit when available and falls back to static catalog only when no DB catalog is wired.
- [ ] Existing gallery ownership and JWT-claims rules remain unchanged.

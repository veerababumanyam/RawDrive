## User story

As a studio owner, when I open /settings/business I immediately see my studio logo presented prominently at the top of the page (a brand-identity header), with a graceful placeholder when no logo is set — matching how SaaS settings pages surface brand identity.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Add a prominent brand-identity header at the top of the business settings page that surfaces the studio logo + brand name, with a placeholder + CTA when absent, wired to the same workspace profile state as the upload flow.
- **Out of scope:** The crop interaction (covered by 1b)
- **Out of scope:** Backend endpoints (covered by 1a)
- **Out of scope:** Public gallery / invoice branding surfaces (unchanged)

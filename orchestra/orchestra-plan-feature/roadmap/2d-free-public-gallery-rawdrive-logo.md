## User story

As a free workspace owner, my publicly shared galleries should carry RawDrive branding; after upgrading to a paid plan, my public galleries should no longer show the RawDrive logo unless the platform explicitly requires it.

## Scope

- **Goal:** Show RawDrive logo on all public/shared free galleries and remove that logo for paid tiers while preserving paid studio branding and enterprise footer hiding.
- **Summary:** Extend the public branding payload with an entitlement-owned platform-branding flag/logo URL and render a token-styled RawDrive logo badge in the existing public gallery footer path only when the backend says it is required.
- **Out of scope:** Stamping a RawDrive watermark over client photos or hero cover images
- **Out of scope:** Changing custom studio logo upload/crop behavior
- **Out of scope:** Changing single-photo share access rules

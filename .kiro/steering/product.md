# RawDrive Product Overview

## What is RawDrive?

RawDrive is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for photographers, studios, and corporate teams to manage the complete media workflow:

- **Ingest & Organize**: Upload photos/videos to managed storage (Cloudflare R2) or BYOS (Google Drive, Dropbox, AWS S3, Azure Blob)
- **Deliver & Collaborate**: Create beautiful galleries with client proofing, selections, comments, and approvals
- **Produce**: Design print and digital albums with lab-ready exports
- **Operate**: Manage clients, bookings, calendar integrations, quotes, and payments (India-first with Razorpay/Stripe)
- **Discover**: AI-powered internal search (GEO) with semantic metadata, embeddings, and face recognition
- **Govern**: Enterprise-grade features including SSO, RBAC, policies, retention rules, and audit logging

## Target Users

1. **Photographers/Studios**: Deliver galleries, sell albums, manage bookings and payments
2. **Clients**: View galleries, select favorites, request edits, approve albums
3. **Corporate Teams**: Secure internal media portals with SSO, policies, and governance
4. **Enterprise Admins**: Manage workspaces, users, retention policies, and compliance

## Core Principles

- **Client experience is the product**: Viewing, selecting, and approving must be effortless on mobile
- **Fast by default**: Progressive loading, caching, and derivatives for instant galleries
- **Trust and control**: Sharing is explicit, auditable, and policy-driven
- **BYOS without pain**: Users keep their storage; RawDrive provides UX, metadata, search, and governance
- **AI that saves time**: Background jobs, clear previews, reversible actions

## Key Features

### Phase 1 (MVP)
- Multi-tenant workspaces with RBAC
- Gallery upload and sharing
- Client portal with masonry grid and lightbox
- Public share links with password protection

### Phase 2-3
- Client selections, comments, and approvals
- Album designer with print presets and exports
- Sub-galleries and advanced sharing controls
- Watermarking and download restrictions

### Phase 4
- CRM and client management
- Booking calendar with conflict detection
- Quotes, invoices (GST-compliant), and payments
- Subscription plans with usage limits

### Phase 5-6
- BYOS storage providers (Google Drive, Dropbox, S3, Azure)
- AI features: quality scoring, face grouping, auto-captions
- Semantic search with embeddings (GEO)
- Background job processing

### Phase 7+
- White-label corporate workspaces
- SAML/OIDC SSO and Just-in-Time provisioning
- Sensitivity labels and retention policies
- Legal hold and immutable retention
- Audit logs and compliance exports

## India-First Approach

- Razorpay as primary payment gateway (UPI, card, netbanking)
- GST-compliant invoicing
- Support for Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu
- RTL rendering for Urdu
- Locale-aware formatting for dates, times, numbers, and currency

## Competitive Advantage

RawDrive combines three capabilities most competitors split across products:
1. Client delivery + proofing
2. Album production
3. Enterprise governance + BYOS + AI discovery

This unified platform reduces friction and provides better value for photographers and enterprises.

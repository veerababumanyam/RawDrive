## Critical Issues (High Priority)

1. **AI Processing Error Visibility** — "Some analyses failed" with no diagnostic info or recovery steps
2. **Feature Discovery Gap** — 14+ AI tools with minimal explanation, no interactive tutorial
3. **Incomplete Settings Guidance** — 11 settings tabs without presets or setup wizard

## Medium Priority Issues

4. Gallery metrics clarity ("Needs Attention 0%" contradictory)
5. Sub-gallery permission model unclear (inherited vs independent)
6. Bulk operations discoverability weak

## New Gaps Identified (Not in Roadmap)

### Gallery-Specific (feed into v1.2)
- **GLTIP-01**: AI tool tooltips with 1-2 sentence descriptions and video links
- **GBULK-01**: Visible bulk action toolbar (batch edit, rename, tag, download)
- **GPRST-01**: Gallery settings presets (Proofing, Delivery, Sharing, Premium Delivery)
- **GSTAT-01**: AI processing progress bar with estimated completion and per-photo status
- **GSMRT-01**: Smart sub-gallery grouping suggestions (by date, EXIF, AI tags)

### Platform-Wide (feed into v1.3/v2+)
- **GTMPL-01**: Gallery templates (Wedding, Event, Product with pre-built structures)
- **GCOLB-01**: Real-time client collaboration (markup tools, approval workflow)
- **GCOMM-01**: Direct print ordering from gallery, album builder, gift options
- **GAILR-01**: Style-based AI recommendations, seasonal suggestions, preference learning

## Business Models Enabled by Gallery

1. **Proofing** — Watermarked, password-protected, limited views
2. **Premium Delivery** — Web-only gallery, originals via separate channel
3. **Self-Service Ordering** — Web-only with selections enabled
4. **Social Sharing** — Unlimited view, no expiration
5. **Client Preview** — Password-protected with favorites tracking

## Recommendations by Timeline

### Short-Term (1-2 sprints): Error messaging, tooltips, settings presets, status visibility
### Medium-Term (1-2 months): Interactive tour, bulk ops, sub-gallery intelligence, analytics
### Long-Term (3-6 months): Templates, collaboration, advanced sharing, commerce, AI enhancements
